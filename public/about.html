Key Engineering & Security Features

1

Multi-Gateway Official APIs

Direct integration with eduboardresults.gov.bd (subject-wise numeric marks) and result.bangladeshgov.org (multi-board gateway covering SSC, HSC, JSC, DIBS across all 11 boards), plus api.ebr.bd lookup support.

2

Light Security Wave Banknote Marksheet

Subtle pastel guilloche curve underprints, microtext security border, geometric rosettes, and official transparent PNG/SVG crests for every Education Board (including Rajshahi Board's official emblem).

3

1-Page A4/Letter QR & HMAC Verification

Every marksheet includes a compressed, HMAC-SHA256 signed QR token (/verify?t=...) that verifies untampered records and prints as a crisp 1-page A4 or Letter certificate.

4

Merit Analytics & 0.01s PDF Scanner

Automatically computes total marks (with and without 4th subject), percentages, and scans uploaded EIIN college result PDFs in milliseconds.

Open Data & Responsible Use

ResultSniper BD never fabricates or alters official examination results. All verified marksheets directly reflect data retrieved from official public Education Board endpoints.
