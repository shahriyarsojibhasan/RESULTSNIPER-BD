Terms of Service

Last Updated: November 2026 • Author: Shahriyar Sojib Hasan

1. Platform Purpose

ResultSniper BD is an independent educational utility designed to help students, parents, and institutions retrieve and format public Bangladesh Education Board results alongside cryptographic verification certificates.

2. Government Data Ownership & Disclaimer

All examination records, candidate names, grades, and marks remain the sole intellectual property of the respective Education Boards of Bangladesh (Dhaka, Rajshahi, Chattogram, Cumilla, Barishal, Sylhet, Jashore, Dinajpur, Mymensingh, Madrasah, and Technical). ResultSniper BD is not an official government portal; printed verification copies are informational aids and do not replace original Board certificates.

3. Cryptographic Verification

Each generated marksheet embeds an HMAC-SHA256 signature validating that its content matches what the upstream Board API returned at issuance time. Any post-issuance edit invalidates verification immediately. In any discrepancy, official Board gazettes govern.

4. Acceptable Use

Users agree not to use this service for automated bulk scraping, DDoS attacks, circumventing anti-bot protections (such as Cloudflare Turnstile), or forging academic records.
