Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Author: Shahriyar Sojib Hasan

1. Zero Permanent Result Storage

ResultSniper BD prioritizes student confidentiality. Roll numbers, registration numbers, names, and examination grades fetched via APIs are never persisted in any remote database or sold to third parties.

2. Browser-Local 7-Day History

Search history (last 7 days) is stored strictly inside your own browser localStorage. You can clear all local history anytime with a single click from the Saved & History tab.

3. Stateless Cryptographic Verification Tokens

QR verification works statelessly using zlib-compressed data signed with HMAC-SHA256. Verification tokens exist inside the generated link/QR code rather than a central tracking database.

4. Ad-Free & Tracker-Free

This platform contains no invasive ad networks, spyware scripts, or third-party marketing trackers.

5. Contact

For privacy or security inquiries, contact creator Shahriyar Sojib Hasan via GitHub.
